## Overview

I took on the design and manufacturing lead role for my team's project in GEEN 1400 Engineering Projects. Our goal was to track how full a bus is by counting riders as they board and to display that count where people can see it.

## Design and fabrication

The system has three parts: an infrared emitter housing that mounts at the door, a transmitter that runs the counting logic on an Arduino, and an LED display that shows the occupancy. I modeled the transmitter and display housings in SolidWorks Sheet Metal, created detailed drawings, and prepared the flat patterns in CorelDRAW so they could be cut and bent in the shop.

## Testing

We used structured verification testing to measure how often the system missed or double-counted a rider. Through iterative changes to both the electrical design and the mechanical mounting, we reduced detection error below 2% and reached 98% detection accuracy. I also applied project management practices with the course professor across the full design cycle.

The project won the section best project award from department leaders.
