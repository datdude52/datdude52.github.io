## Overview

This was my individual project for MCEN 1025 CAD & Fabrication. The goal was to reverse-engineer a commercial product, and I chose a Honeywell desk fan.

## Process

I disassembled the fan and captured dimensional data with calipers and angle finders, paying close attention to the features where parts meet. I modeled each part in SolidWorks and built the rotating system as a five-part assembly, with roughly 130 features across the two most complex housings. Throughout the project I focused on dimensional accuracy and tolerance awareness so the parts would fit together the way the original parts do.

I finished by producing engineering drawings that document the mechanical architecture and assembly relationships.
